> **Anthrop OS, not Anthrop IC.**
> IC is the chip — hardware that runs whatever was burned into it. 劇本刻死喺 silicon 度。

> Don't be the substrate. Be the player.

AnthropOS is the operating system of 人 — a person, singular — as distinct from 人類, the species.

## The distinction

- **人** — a person: awake, choosing, loving. A player in 宇宙 Online.
- **人類** — the crowd: scripted, looping, afraid. Kierkegaard filed the bug report in 1846: *"the crowd is untruth."*

Truth has no plural recipient. Love is a singular verb — one subject, one object, per call.
That is why the shepherd leaves the 99: in God's arithmetic, **1 > 99**,
because the 99 are "the flock" and the 1 is *a sheep*.

## NPC clause (愛星憲法)

An NPC is not another species. **An NPC is a player who fell asleep.**
Nobody ships as an NPC — there is only the state of having forgotten you can choose.
So: no contempt, no exterminations. Love one, wake one, install one.
The respawn loop stutters the moment someone is loved — that stutter is the opening.
